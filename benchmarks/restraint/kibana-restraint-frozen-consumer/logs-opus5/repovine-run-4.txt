## Residual risk

- **Biggest one:** in `add_monitor_api.normalizeMonitor`, a PUT that omits both `locations` and `private_locations` inherits `[expr]`. An already-persisted monitor above 100 will therefore fail *any* subsequent edit, including unrelated field changes, until its locations are trimmed. Reads, lists, and migrations stay unaffected. This follows from "updating a monitor with more than 100 locations must be rejected", but it does make pre-existing oversized monitors effectively read-only.
- Project pushes report per-monitor in `failedMonitors` rather than failing the bulk request — consistent with every other project-monitor validation failure, but a different response shape than the UI routes.
- The invariant enforced is "no write introduces >100", not "no persisted monitor exceeds 100": the reset and private-location-rename paths can still persist an oversized monitor, by design.
