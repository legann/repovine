Message: *"A monitor can target at most 100 locations, but N were configured. Remove locations from this monitor, or split it into multiple monitors."*

A flag defaults correctly but still couples a frozen consumer to future edits. I reverted it; `monitor_validation.ts` is untouched, so no existing rule changed and no ordering shifted.
