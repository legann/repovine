## Changed behavior

A monitor may target at most **100** locations. Create/update writes with more are rejected with a 400: reason `"Monitor targets too many locations"`, details naming the actual count and the cap. The count is the **normalized, deduplicated** location set — `getMonitorLocations` returns `uniqBy(…)` (`normalizers/common_fields.ts:268`), so a public+private overlap isn't double-counted.
