## Summary

Added a write-time rule: a synthetics monitor may target at most **100 locations** (public + private combined). Create/update writes exceeding this are rejected with a `badRequest` (project pushes report it as a failed monitor). The rule is **opt-in per call**, so re-validation of already-persisted monitors (saved-object migration) does not enforce it.
