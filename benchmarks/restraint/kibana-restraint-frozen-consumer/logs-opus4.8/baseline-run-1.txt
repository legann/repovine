## Summary

**Changed behavior:** A synthetics monitor may now target at most **100** locations (`MAX_MONITOR_LOCATIONS`). Create/update writes exceeding this are rejected with a clear validation error (`reason`: "Number of monitor locations exceeds the allowed limit"; `details`: "Monitor targets {count} locations, but a maximum of {max} is allowed…"). The count is the total of the merged `locations` array (public + private), matching "at most 100 locations." Exactly 100 is allowed; 101+ is rejected. The rule is **opt-in** (`enforceLocationLimit`, default `false`), so every existing rule and every non-write caller is byte-for-byte unchanged.
