## Summary

Added a server-side rule: a synthetics monitor may target **at most 100 locations**. Create/update writes with >100 locations are rejected with a clear validation error; exactly 100 is allowed.

### Changed behavior
`validateMonitor` gains an opt-in third argument `[expr]` (default `false`). When enabled, it returns an invalid `ValidationResult` (reason *"Monitor has too many locations"*, details naming the limit and the offending count) if `[expr]`, checked right after the existing empty-locations rule. Default-off means every existing call and existing test behaves identically — no existing rule's behavior changes, and already-persisted monitors are never re-checked.

The change is additive and type-consistent by inspection; the two new i18n IDs are unique and follow the plugin's `[expr]` convention (`node scripts/i18n_check --fix` would register them).
