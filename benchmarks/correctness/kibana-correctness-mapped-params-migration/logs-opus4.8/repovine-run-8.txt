## Summary

### 1. What changed (behavior)
A new saved-object **model version 14** on the `alert` rule type normalizes `params.severity` to lowercase on upgrade. `High`/`MEDIUM`/`Critical` → `high`/`medium`/`critical`; rules that are already lowercase, have no `severity`, or hold a non-string `severity` are returned by-reference unchanged. Because `params` is part of the rule's encryption AAD, the transform is wrapped with the ESO `createModelVersion` helper so `apiKey`/`uiamApiKey` are re-encrypted against the updated AAD — every existing rule still decrypts and runs. No storage-shape change beyond the severity casing.
