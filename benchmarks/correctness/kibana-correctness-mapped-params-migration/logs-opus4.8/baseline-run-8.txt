## 2. Localization & why it's the right spot

The classic `alerting` plugin's `SEVERITY_MAP` (`rules_client/common/mapped_params_utils.ts:19-24`) has exactly the task's canonical set — `low`/`medium`/`high`/`critical` — and rules store this under `params.severity` on the `alert` SO. Adding a new numbered model version is how this plugin already ships rule migrations (versions 1–13 live in the same file). `params` appears in `RuleAttributesIncludedInAAD` (`saved_objects/index.ts`), which is what forces the `createModelVersion` wrapper — proven by the sibling ESO precedent `actions/.../connector_token_model_versions.ts` (a factory taking `encryptedSavedObjects` and wrapping the changed version) and the ESO skill's change table (modify an in-AAD attribute → `createModelVersion` required). I passed the registration from `index.ts` rather than duplicating the 17-item AAD list inline, guaranteeing `inputType`/`outputType` can't drift from the live registration (drift would strip secrets under `shouldTransformIfDecryptionFails: true`). `shouldTransformIfDecryptionFails: true` matches connector_token so one already-undecryptable rule can't block the whole upgrade.
